# Status of the health "I don't know" project, 29 September 2026

## Summary

All four planned experiments ran between 18 and 20 September 2026, and no model run has happened since. The branch `health-idk-gate` has 24 commits and no uncommitted changes. Results are final for this round. The full write-up is `docs/report/2026-09-19-health-idk-report.md`.

The main results, all on Llama 3.1 8B Instruct:

1. A gate outside the model met the 10% error target on held-out data. It answered 31.2% of 2,388 test questions, 5.9% of those answers were wrong (95% upper bound 7.5%), and it refused all 297 invented-drug and invented-disease questions. With no gate, 37.7% of answers were wrong.
2. The model's own "I don't know" follows whether it recognizes the drug or disease name. It picks "I don't know" on 63% of invented-entity questions and on 2% of real questions it answers wrongly. Semantic entropy is about the same for both groups (median 0.74 and 0.75 nats).
3. Two attention heads, L15.H4 and L17.H25, account for 51.0% of the abstention signal on 99 held-out pairs. Random pairs of heads account for -1.9%.
4. Adding a push along those two heads when semantic entropy is high makes the model reply "I don't know" by itself. With one threshold this matches a plain entropy cut-off. With up to three thresholds plus a trigger on the heads' own output, utility rises by 0.046 [0.020, 0.071] when a wrong answer costs 4 times a right one. No gain was found when both cost the same.

Terms used above: *semantic entropy* is the spread of the model's probability over the four answer letters (0 means certain, 1.386 nats means uniform). *Abstention signal* is the log-probability of "I don't know" minus that of any letter. *Utility* is right answers minus cost times wrong answers, divided by the number of questions.

## What was built

The code in `code/` implements the research design in `../uncertainty-mech-interp/` (RFC Version 1.3). The CLI has four commands: `run` builds and certifies the gate, `ask` answers demonstration questions with a frozen gate, `circuits` runs the patching and steering study, and `tiers` runs tiered steering from saved vectors. Twelve end-to-end tests run every command on fake models in about 10 seconds.

The data are 10,000 MedQA exam questions with four options and 1,500 questions about 500 invented entities, where every answer is counted wrong. Questions are split by group into discovery, probability calibration, threshold certification and test roles, and the test role was scored once per experiment.

## Results by experiment

| Experiment | Question | Result | Run directory |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Certified gate, pilot | Can 4,600 questions certify a 10% target? | No. 427 certification units gave a lowest bound of 15.9%, so the gate answered nothing | `runs/health-llama31-8b-test-run` |
| 1. Certified gate, second run | Same, with 11,500 questions | Yes, at threshold 0.15 (see summary point 1) | `runs/health-llama31-8b-full-run` |
| 2. Abstention circuit | Which components produce "I don't know"? | Heads L15.H4 and L17.H25; head L30.H25 has an effect of -51% | `runs/circuit-se-steering` |
| 3. Entropy-gated steering | Does steering beat an entropy cut-off? | Equal on real questions; invented refusal 86.9% against 73.4% | `runs/circuit-se-steering` |
| 4. Tiered steering | Do several thresholds help? | Yes at costs 2 and 4 (+0.034 and +0.046 utility), no difference at cost 1 | `runs/tiered-se-steering` |

Three results limit what the first experiment shows:

- On real MedQA questions alone, the activation probe ranked errors no better than output statistics (AUROC 0.781 against 0.796). The probe's advantage comes from separating invented from real questions.
- Adding one wrong-minus-right direction at layer 19 did not raise "I don't know" above random directions of the same size (0.084 against 0.093), and it cut accuracy from 67.6% to 55.3%.
- After the run, threshold 0.07 met a 5% bound on the certification data (588 answered, 14 wrong, bound 4.7%). This was not tested on the test role.

## Open issues

- **Stale RFC files.** `../uncertainty-mech-interp/README.md` still says "The project contains no completed model experiment", and `VALIDATION.md` still lists Jupyter repair as a blocker. The experiments ran from the `code/` CLI, not the notebooks.
- **Deviations from the RFC.** The model is Llama 3.1 8B, because Gemma 2 access returned HTTP 403. The target is 10% instead of 5%. Answers are letters, not free text. SAE features, path patching and TruthfulQA were not run.
- **Evidence strength.** The second run reuses questions from the pilot, so it counts as a larger test run, not a confirmatory one. Invented names differ from real ones in both familiarity and spelling (real drug names carry class suffixes such as -pril), and the pairs cannot tell these apart.

## Candidate next steps

Each item below would need its own design document in `docs/specs/` before an implementation plan.

1. **Path patching in layers 11 to 16.** Trace how the entity information reaches the final token, head by head. Output: which heads move information from the entity to the instruction tail.
2. **Ablate head L30.H25.** Test whether removing it raises "I don't know". Small change to the `circuits` command; about 10 minutes of GPU time.
3. **Certify at 5%.** Freeze threshold 0.07 in advance and score it on fresh MedQA questions. Needs questions outside the current 10,176.
4. **Free-text answers.** Apply tiered steering to generated answers, with semantic entropy from sampled answers and a clustering step. Largest change of the four, because grading needs a judge.
5. **Second model and fresh questions.** Repeat on another model as the confirmatory run. Gemma 2 needs the licence accepted on the Hugging Face account first.
